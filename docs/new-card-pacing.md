# New-Card Pacing (MVP)

Adaptive Review introduces at most five new cards per student, activity, and
student-local calendar day.

Due reviews always take priority. While at least one card is due in an
activity, its New queue is paused. Completing the due queue makes any remaining
daily new-card allowance available immediately.

The limit counts distinct cards first attempted in New sessions. Both correct
and incorrect first attempts count as introductions, and append-only attempt
history prevents a progress reset or page refresh from bypassing the limit.

Pacing affects only the New queue. Students can still open All Cards and browse
the complete set without changing mastery or consuming the daily allowance.

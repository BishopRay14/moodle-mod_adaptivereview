# New-Card Access (MVP)

Adaptive Review does not cap the number of new cards a student may begin in a
day or session.

Due reviews take priority. While at least one card is due in an activity, its
New queue is paused. Completing the due queue makes every unstarted card in the
set available immediately.

This policy is enforced when an answer is recorded, so a page refresh or direct
request cannot begin new material while reviews are due.

Students can always open All Cards and browse the complete set without changing
mastery.

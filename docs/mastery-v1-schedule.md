# Mastery-v1 Schedule

Date approved: 2026-10-15

## Purpose

The MVP schedule balances two goals:

1. A student can bring a staggered 40–50-card set to at least 3.0 during a
   12–16 week semester.
2. Higher mastery requires successful retrieval across months and eventually
   years rather than rapid repeated practice.

## Approved intervals

The interval is selected from the card's mastery after a successful review.

| Mastery after review | Next required interval |
| --- | ---: |
| 0.5–0.9 | 1 day |
| 1.0–1.9 | 3 days |
| 2.0–2.4 | 7 days |
| 2.5–2.9 | 15 days |
| 3.0–3.4 | 21 days |
| 3.5–3.9 | 30 days |
| 4.0–4.4 | 60 days |
| 4.5–4.9 | 90 days |
| 5.0–5.4 | 150 days |
| 5.5–5.9 | 270 days |

A new card begins at 0.0. Its first successful retrieval raises it to 0.5 and
schedules the one-day interval. A card at 5.9 continues to receive 270-day
reviews and is never permanently finished.

## Timing expectations

With perfect on-time retrieval, a new card reaches 3.0 about 29 days after its
first successful review. Real progress normally takes longer because late
reviews move the future schedule from the actual review time and unsuccessful
reviews insert recovery intervals without reducing mastery.

## Early and recovery reviews

Early success earns a proportional gain based on elapsed time, capped at 0.5.
Incorrect responses earn no mastery and schedule a recovery at one third of the
active interval, rounded up to a minimum of one day. Repeated failures continue
shortening the active recovery interval. Same-session remediation is recorded
but remains ineligible for mastery gain.
